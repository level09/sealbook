Outside assistants

An outside assistant, such as a chat app on your computer, can connect to Sealbook over MCP and work for you. It searches and reads evidence and proposes facts and same-person links for review, with the access you approve and never beyond your role. Every read is logged, nothing it proposes is accepted without a person, and disconnecting it stops it on its next call.

Connect one

  1. In the assistant, add a remote MCP server with this address: ${ mcp.url }
  2. The assistant opens Sealbook in your browser. Sign in if asked.
  3. Read what it asks to do and press Approve.
  4. It appears in the list below, acting as the assistant for you.
