768-dimensional space.

Migration head: `20261027_research`, following `20261026_geotag`. Apply migrations only to an approved target environment. Then use
`stk enrich` with its printed `--after` cursor and `stk worker` to populate the
indexes. Semantic paths stay disabled until their model settings are supplied.

handwritten records and field audio.
