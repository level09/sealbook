The footage that was two years old

On the morning of 2 June 2026 a clip of the river Ostra over its banks in Lindmark spreads under the caption “JUST IN”. A newsroom's tip line holds 36 clips from the same days, many of them brown water under grey skies, and has to say which ones are new.

Fictional case of verification. The town, the river, the accounts, the captions and the dates are invented. The footage is real, openly licensed and filmed elsewhere.

The story

The viral clip carries a red banner and a logo block and runs 14 seconds. Put beside the older clips in the pile, its picture matches none of them.

It is a cropped, mirrored, trimmed and re-encoded copy of a video posted on 3 June 2024. The picture no longer matches the original. The sound still does.

The pile

36 clips of real footage: one 21 second original, nine copies made from it, six other real recordings of rivers in flood posted on the same days with similar captions, and twenty unrelated clips of clouds, rain, snow, waterfalls, a rail line and a fountain.

What the machine found

Each clip gets a picture fingerprint and a sound fingerprint. A low-rate copy and a trimmed copy kept both. The logo, the crop, the mirror and an upright cut defeated the picture fingerprint, and the sound carried the match. A muted copy of the viral clip matched it by picture, and through it the original.

The tool also listed 36 weak matches outside the family, each resting on one or two shared moments of sound. They are marked weak.

What the person decided

A person plays the two clips side by side, hears the same water and sees the same bush mirrored, and confirms the match. The finding says the clip is two years old and cites both files. The weak matches are declined.

The score

8 of 9 planted copies tied to the original.

All eight are banded strong, and no strong match falls outside the planted family. Sound did most of the work: five of the eight were found by sound alone.

Measured on 21 November 2026 on this synthetic case by a scoring script that runs the app's fingerprint code over the 36 clips, with exact search in place of the database. A test in the source pins the result.

The limit

A copy cut upright, mirrored and with its sound removed is not found. The picture fingerprint does not survive a crop or a mirror, and with no sound left there is nothing to match.
